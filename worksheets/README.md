# The worksheet parts cited by the paper

| Part | file | contents |
|---|---|---|
| W1--W4 | `W1-W4-worksheets.pdf` (source `W1-W4-worksheets.tex`) | the principal example at p = 5 in full: class fields and secondary norm operators (W1), the transversality computation (W2), the closed points of the norm-degeneracy scheme (W3), one certificate entry restated in the notation of the paper (W4) |
| W5 | `W5-tables-p5.pdf` (source `W5-tables-p5.tex`, generated by `generate_tables_p5.py`) | the table of the 204 fields at p = 5 with their transverse elements |
| W6 | `W6-tables-p3.pdf` (source `W6-tables-p3.tex`, generated by `generate_tables_p3.py`) | the tables of the 864 mild fields at p = 3 decided in the paper, with the route for each |
| W7 | `part-W7.tsv` | the index of the fields decided after the paper (version 3): one line per field with prime, rank, coefficient field of the change of variables and the path of its certificate |

`certificate-guide.pdf` is the human-readable companion of one certificate, generated by `tools/certificate_guide.gp`.
