\section*{Notation and conventions}
\textbf{Ideals as matrices.}  An ideal of the ring of integers is
stored by its \emph{Hermite normal form} (HNF): an upper-triangular
integer matrix whose columns are the coordinates, with respect to
the fixed integral basis, of a $\mathbf Z$-basis of the ideal.
For example, the ideal $J=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}256&21\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ of Entry~1 below, over $K$ with
integral basis $(1,s)$, denotes the ideal $\mathbf Z\,256+\mathbf Z\,(21+s)$.  The
determinant of the matrix is the norm of the ideal.  Elements are
likewise given by their coordinate vectors in the integral basis.
\par\medskip
\textbf{Characters.}  The header fixes three generators
$\kappa_1,\kappa_2,\kappa_3$ of $\operatorname{Cl}(K)$ (as HNF
ideals).  A character $x$ on $\operatorname{Cl}(K)/5$ is recorded
by its value vector $(x(\bar\kappa_1),x(\bar\kappa_2),
x(\bar\kappa_3))$; thus $(1,0,0)$ is the character dual to
$\bar\kappa_1$.  The column number $j$ records which basis
element $e_j$ of $\operatorname{Cl}(K)[5]$ the stored pair
$(a',J)$ represents.
\par\medskip
\textbf{Automorphisms.}  $\sigma_x$ is a field automorphism of
$L_x$.  Writing $\theta$ for the chosen root of the stored
absolute polynomial, so that $L_x=\mathbf Q(\theta)$, the
automorphism is determined by the single value
$\sigma_x(\theta)$, and the certificate stores the coordinate
vector of $\sigma_x(\theta)$ in the integral basis of $L_x$.
