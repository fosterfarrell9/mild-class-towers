# Flag certificates: format and verification

Each file `<|D|>.json` certifies that the imaginary quadratic field of
discriminant `D`, taken from the census of fields with p-class rank 3
whose mildness the paper left undecided, has a mild p-class tower
group of cohomological dimension 2.  The directory
`records/p3/flag-certificates/` holds the certificates at p = 3; the
single certificate at p = 5, for D = -781922404, is
`records/p5/flag-certificates/781922404.json` and has the same format
(see the last section).

## What a certificate contains

| key | meaning |
|---|---|
| `discriminant` | the field discriminant `D` |
| `field` | the coefficient field `k` of the change of variables: `q = p^e` (`p = 3` or `5`, the prime of the census), its `degree` `e`, and the minimal polynomial of its generator `t`, coefficients listed from the constant term upwards (`modulus_low_to_high`); for `e = 1` the entry is a placeholder |
| `basis` | the invertible linear change of variables `g`, a 3-by-3 matrix over `k`; row convention `phi(X_i) = sum_a basis[i][a] * X_a`; entries are integers for `e = 1` and coefficient lists `[c_0, ..., c_{e-1}]` meaning `c_0 + c_1 t + ... + c_{e-1} t^{e-1}` otherwise |
| `head_words` | a finite list of words in the letters `1, 2, 3` (digit `i` stands for `X_i`); the claim is that these are exactly the head words of a finite Groebner basis of the transformed relation ideal, for the graded order that refines degree by the letter order `X_1 > X_2 > X_3` (Singular's `Dp`) |
| `proper_prefixes` | the number `s` of proper prefixes of the head words, the empty word included; the word counts below satisfy a linear recurrence of order at most `s` |
| `search` | provenance: how the change of variables was found, and the truncation degree of the search; this is bookkeeping and carries no logical weight |
| `claim` | a human-readable summary of what the certificate establishes; the verifier does not interpret this text --- `verified` refers to the mathematical fields `field`, `basis`, `head_words` and `proper_prefixes` together with the relation tensor, and means exactly the six steps below |
| `alternatives` | how many further certificates for the same field the search produced (not included) |

The extension fields are represented as `F_p[t]/(m)` with the
following minimal polynomials `m` (the same representation is used by
the verifier; Singular's default polynomials differ for some degrees,
so a comparison with Singular must either pass `m` as `minpoly` or
translate along an explicit isomorphism):

| `p` | `e` | `q` | `m` |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 2 | 9 | `t^2 + 2t + 2` |
| 3 | 3 | 27 | `t^3 + 2t + 1` |
| 3 | 4 | 81 | `t^4 + t + 2` |
| 3 | 5 | 243 | `t^5 + 2t + 1` |
| 3 | 6 | 729 | `t^6 + t + 2` |
| 3 | 7 | 2187 | `t^7 + 2t^2 + 1` |
| 3 | 8 | 6561 | `t^8 + t^3 + 2` |
| 5 | 2 | 25 | `t^2 + 3` |

The cubic relations themselves are not repeated here: they are the
relation tensors of the census, stored with the verified records of
the paper (`records/p3/results/verification-004.json`, `verification-005.json`, `slice2/verification-starthinker.json`, `block23-verification-starthinker.json`, key
`tensor_3_by_27`; row `l`, position `9i + 3j + k` is the coefficient
of `X_{i+1} X_{j+1} X_{k+1}` in the relation `rho_{l+1}`).  At p = 5 the
tensor is the entry `cubic_relation_matrix` of
`records/p5/D-781922404/result.gp`, in the same layout.

## Why the claim implies mildness

Terminology: what the paper calls the *high term* `HT(f)` of an
element `f` we call its *head word*; `HT(I)` is the set of high terms
of the elements of the ideal `I`.

Write `rho_1, rho_2, rho_3` for the census relations in
`A_0 = F_3<X_1, X_2, X_3>` and `I_0` for the ideal they generate;
write `r_1, r_2, r_3` for their images under the change of variables
`phi` given by `basis`, `A = A_0 (x) k` for the free algebra over the
coefficient field `k`, `I` for the ideal generated by `r_1, r_2, r_3`
in `A`, and `m` for the largest degree of a head word. Every step
below applies a statement of the paper to `r_1, r_2, r_3` over `k`
(for Appendix A in the transferred form explained in step 4).

1. **Head words** (Lemma A.5, *Bases from resolved overlaps*, part
   (ii)). The verifier reduces `r_1, r_2, r_3` to head coefficient one
   with distinct head words and then runs the completion of
   Algorithm A.6: for every *overlap* — a pair of head words `AB`,
   `BC` of the current generating set `G` with nonempty words `A, B,
   C` — it reduces `gC - Ag'` with respect to `G`; a nonzero remainder
   is added to `G`. Every overlap of two head words of degree at most
   `m` has degree at most `2m - 1` (the overlapped word `ABC` is
   shorter than the concatenation), so the completion stops there.
   If at the end every overlap reduces to zero and the head words of
   `G` are exactly `head_words`, Lemma A.5 (ii) gives
   `HT(I) = (head_words)`.
2. **Normal words** (Lemma A.1 and Corollary A.3 (ii)). Hence the
   words with no factor in `head_words` are exactly the `I`-normal
   words, and their number `c_n` in degree `n` equals `dim_k (A/I)_n`.
3. **Finitely many degrees** (Lemma A.4, *Word counts satisfy a linear
   recurrence*). With `s` the number of proper prefixes of the head
   words (empty word included), `(c_n)` satisfies a linear recurrence
   of order at most `s`, and the coefficients `(b_n)` of
   `1/(1 - 3z + 3z^3)` one of order 3; hence `c_n = b_n` for
   `n <= s + 2` implies `c_n = b_n` for all `n`. The verifier checks
   the window `n <= s + 3`, one degree more than required.
4. **Strong freeness over k** (Corollary A.2, *Word counts decide
   strong freeness*). Equality of the normal-word counts with the
   coefficients of `1/(1 - 3z + 3z^3)` in every degree is equivalent
   to strong freeness of `r_1, r_2, r_3` over `k`. (Appendix A is
   stated over the prime field; the paper notes after the definition
   of strong freeness in Section 3 that the notion extends to a
   finite extension `k`, and the proofs of the appendix are plain
   linear algebra over the field of coefficients, so they carry over
   to `k` word for word. It is these transferred statements that are
   used here and in the verifier.)
5. **Back to the census relations** (Section 3 of the paper). The
   change of variables `phi` is a graded algebra automorphism of
   `A`; it maps the ideal of `rho_1 (x) 1, rho_2 (x) 1, rho_3 (x) 1`
   onto `I` and preserves the Hilbert series, so
   `rho_1, rho_2, rho_3` are strongly free over `k` (the remark
   following Proposition 3.8 uses the same observation). By
   Lemma 3.7 (*Strong freeness under scalar extension*),
   `dim_k (A/I')_n = dim_{F_3} (A_0/I_0)_n` for the ideal `I'`
   generated by the scalar extension, so `rho_1, rho_2, rho_3` are
   strongly free over `F_3`. For `q = 3` this step is void.
6. **Mildness** (Proposition 3.8, *Mildness from strongly free cubic
   initial forms*). The census relations are the cubic initial forms
   of a minimal set of relators of a minimal presentation
   `1 -> R -> F -> G -> 1` with `R` contained in the third Zassenhaus
   filtration step, as established for every census field in
   Section 4 of the paper (which works with fields of p-class rank
   three); hence `G` is mild and `cd G = 2`.

For the certificates at p = 3 and p = 5, rank three, nothing beyond
the statements of the paper enters.  The rank-four certificates need
one arithmetic input that the paper does not provide; the section on
rank four states it explicitly.

## The certificate at p = 5

The field D = -781922404 is the one field of 5-class rank three with
|D_K| < 2^30 for which the paper claims nothing: its norm-degeneracy
scheme consists of a single non-reduced closed point of degree two, so
the transversality criterion provably cannot apply.  The certificate
`records/p5/flag-certificates/781922404.json` has the format above
with `p = 5`, `e = 2`, `F_25 = F_5[t]/(t^2 + 3)`, and the four head
words `113, 122, 133, 1232` (`s = 6`).  Steps 1 to 6 apply verbatim,
with Appendix A transferred to the coefficient field as in step 4;
Lemma 3.7 and Proposition 3.8 are stated for any odd prime, and
Section 4, which establishes the premise of step 6, works for any odd
prime at p-class rank three.  At p = 5 the three cubic initial forms
are Lie elements (there is no cube term, the p-th power contributing
only in degree 5), as the description of the relator space at p > 3
in Section 3 of the paper predicts.  Independently of that, the
exhaustive search over all 186 flags of F_5^3 found no change of
variables over F_5 itself that terminates by degree 11, so the
extension field is genuinely needed.  The line of the flag (column 1 of `basis`, up to Frobenius)
is exactly the non-reduced point of the norm-degeneracy scheme.

## The certificate of rank four

The field D = -653329427 is the single field of the range with 3-class
rank four.  Its tower group has four generators and four cubic initial
relations; the target series becomes `1/(1 - 4z + 4z^3)` (Corollary A.2
with `d = 4`), words are written in the letters `1, 2, 3, 4`, and the
change of variables is a matrix in `GL_4(F_3)`.  The certificate
`records/p3/flag-certificates-rank-four/653329427.json` has the format
above with the additional key `rank` (`4`); its relation tensor is
`records/p3/rank-four-653329427/tensor.json`, the tensor reconstructed
by `tools/verify_certificate_general.gp` from the witness certificate
of that directory (row `l`, position `16i + 4j + k`).

Steps 1 to 5 apply with `d = 4` throughout: the reconstruction of the
cubic initial forms from the rows of `T` is stated in the paper for
any odd prime and any rank, strong freeness and the series
`1/(1 - dz + dz^3)` are defined and characterised for general `d`
(Corollary A.2 with `d` generators and `d` cubic relations), and
Proposition 3.8 and Lemma 3.7 are stated for any rank.  Step 6 is the
one point where the paper does not carry the rank-four case: its
Section 4, which establishes for every census field that the tensor
rows are the cubic initial forms of a minimal set of relators, assumes
p-class rank three.  For rank four this arithmetic premise is
established by the witness certificate lying next to the tensor: the
`certificate.gp` of the directory extends the certificate layer of the
paper's verification appendix to rank four (standard character family
with explicit norm witnesses, 40 entries), it is checked by the
independent pure-GP verifier `tools/verify_certificate_general.gp`
(812 checks; the base class group made unconditional by `bnfcertify`),
and the tensor that verifier reconstructs is `tensor.json` --- the
rank-generic flag verifier `tools/verify_flag_certificate_d.py`
checks that discriminant, prime and rank of the certificate agree with
`tensor.json`, that `tensor.json` is the tensor printed in
`verification.log`, and that `certificate.gp` belongs to the same
field, so that the flag certificate and the arithmetic certificate of
a directory are always read together.  This is the same
certificate layer that certified the three rank-four fields announced
on 27 August.

The complete rank-four census lives under
`records/p3/rank-four-census/`: `manifest.tsv` lists all 63 known
fields of 3-class rank four (62 with |D| < 2 * 10^10, enumerated
independently by Belabas's cubic counts and the Mosunov--Jacobson
tables, plus D = -20217903567), and each flag-decided field has a
directory `K-<|D|>/` with `tensor.json` (the relation tensor
reconstructed by the witness verifier), the flag certificate
`<|D|>.json` in the format above, the witness certificate
`certificate.gp`, and its `verification.log` (812 checks each).  The
verifier resolves the tensor of a census certificate from that
directory automatically.

## How to verify

    python3 tools/verify_flag_certificate.py records/p3/flag-certificates/<certificate>.json
    python3 tools/verify_flag_certificate.py records/p5/flag-certificates/781922404.json
    python3 tools/verify_flag_certificate_d.py records/p3/flag-certificates-rank-four/653329427.json

The verifier is plain Python (no computer algebra system); it loads
the relation tensor from the census records, applies the change of
variables, and performs steps 1 and 2 above from scratch. Exit code 0
means verified. `WORKED-EXAMPLE.md` carries out every step of the
smallest certificate by hand.
